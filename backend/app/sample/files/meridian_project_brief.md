# Project Meridian — Product Brief & Business Case

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT created for the ASK//WHY demonstration. All companies, products, figures and people are fictional.

Owner: Product Strategy. Version 1.4, last updated 2 September 2026.

## 1. Summary

Meridian is a mobile scheduling and dispatch app for small HVAC and plumbing contractors with 3 to 25 technicians. It replaces whiteboards and phone calls with optimized daily routes, automated customer reminders and same-day job updates.

## 2. Goals

- Public launch on 15 March 2027, ahead of the spring maintenance season.
- Reach 2,500 paying accounts within 12 months of launch.
- Achieve $2.37M in annual recurring revenue by month 12 at an average of $79 per account per month.

## 3. Growth plan

We assume paying accounts will grow 35% month-over-month from the 120 pilot accounts we already have, driven by contractor referrals and a partnership with regional supply distributors.

Pilot customers love the product, so conversion from pilot to paid should be close to 100%.

Customer acquisition cost is expected to stay below $180 per account because most growth will come from referrals.

## 4. Scope

The MVP will be online-only. Offline support is deferred to v2 because technicians generally have reliable mobile coverage.

Route optimization will be provided by the PathCore Routing API. Customer SMS reminders will be sent through our existing messaging provider.

Support will be handled by the existing two-person customer success team.

## 5. Budget

Total approved project budget: $1.2M, covering engineering, design, launch marketing and the first year of infrastructure.

## 6. Risks

The main risk is competitor pricing. We will respond with a lower introductory price if needed.
