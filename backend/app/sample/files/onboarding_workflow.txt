MERIDIAN CUSTOMER ONBOARDING WORKFLOW v0.3
SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT created for the ASK//WHY demonstration. All details are fictional.

Purpose: describe how a new paying account goes from signed contract to first dispatched job.

Step 1. Sales marks the account as closed-won in the CRM.
Step 2. Customer success schedules a 60-minute onboarding call within 48 hours of signup.
Step 3. Before the call, a customer success manager spends 30 minutes importing the customer's technician list and service areas.
Step 4. During the call, the customer success manager configures routing preferences and sends a test SMS reminder.
Step 5. The account is marked live once the first job is dispatched through Meridian.

Ownership: every step after Step 1 is owned by the customer success team.
Service level: onboarding calls must happen within 48 hours of signup for every new account.
Escalation: if an onboarding call is missed, the account owner is notified by email.
Each customer success manager works 140 productive hours per month, shared between onboarding and support tickets.
